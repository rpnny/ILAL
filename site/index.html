One protocol. Three capabilities.

ILAL connects institutional access and execution in one atomic settlement path.

Eligibility

CNF credentials and ZK policy proofs establish access. Short-lived grants reuse eligibility within a pool’s current policy, while every transaction retains explicit authorization.

Execution

Signed orders match internally. Only the remaining imbalance reaches the AMM. Price, output and exposure bounds are enforced on-chain in one transaction.

Your liquidity stays yours

Positions and fees belong to their owners. Adding liquidity requires eligibility. Withdrawing and collecting remain available after grant expiry or policy shutdown.

Use the local application

Build the CLI from the repository, then start the wallet-based console:

node cli/dist/index.js console \
  --manifest deployments/base-sepolia/v1.0.0-mixed-testnet.1.json \
  --rpc https://sepolia.base.org

Open http://127.0.0.1:4174. Your wallet signs every action.
