Issuer Stablecoin ↔ Settlement Cash

The issuer defines eligibility for the ILAL pool involving Asset A. A separate sandbox operator controls the USDC-like settlement asset. Eligible institutions keep custody and sign bounded orders.

Execution-time policy

A valid quote is not authorization. Credentials, grants, policy revision, signatures, limits, balances and allowances are checked again in the atomic execution transaction.

Owner-safe liquidity

Eligibility controls new risk-taking actions. Credential revocation, policy shutdown and oracle failure cannot trap LP principal or earned fees.

Reproduce the pilot

make pilot-test

The isolated gate deploys from a blank chain, exercises separately controlled roles, proves the 170/140/30 decomposition, tests quote → policy change → execute rollback, and verifies LP exit after policy failure.
